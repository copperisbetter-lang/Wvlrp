Tier 3 / Featured partnership

Your partnership includes.

  • Dedicated Roost page and featured naming placement.
  • Prominent paired logos in the 360° experience.
  • Direct station audio and clear listening controls.
  • Featured contest panel and DJ-led participation.
  • Station app promotion and official show-profile links.
  • Space for agreed station events and promotions.
  • Monthly reporting on visits, repeat visits, time spent, and partner-link clicks.

Proposed package. Pricing, audio arrangements, contest operations, and reporting setup will be agreed together.

Built to work both ways

On air.
In the clearing.

98.7 introduces listeners to The Roost through agreed on-air mentions and DJ participation. WVLRP features The Mountain throughout its dedicated Roost experience.

More ways to connect

Station events, merchandise, and approved prizes can make the partnership feel local and personal. Existing station commercials remain part of its broadcast; separate visual advertiser placements can be coordinated with the station.

See what the partnership earns

Agreed reporting would show how visitors use the page and which station links they follow. The showcase makes no audience or revenue guarantees.
