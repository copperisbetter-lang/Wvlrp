THE ROOST × 98.7 THE MOUNTAIN

A West Virginia wildlife experience with a West Virginia soundtrack — built to give 98.7 a natural place inside The Roost without getting between viewers and the wildlife they came to see.

WVLRP • WE DO IT LIVE

Why 98.7 fits The Roost

The Roost is a live wildlife camera, but audio is part of the environment too. Our concept is to carry 98.7 The Mountain with The Roost experience, giving viewers music while they watch and giving the station a recurring presence in a place people may leave open for long stretches of time.

The radio also serves a practical purpose around the Roost: human voices and changing sound can help make the area less comfortable for predators around the chickens. That means the station is not being dropped onto the page as a random advertisement — it has a reason to be there.

Not an ad pasted over a camera.
A partnership built into the experience.
24/7 PRESENCE

98.7 can become the soundtrack associated with The Roost whenever the live audio experience is active.

ZERO VIDEO INTERRUPTION

No pop-ups over animals. No pre-roll blocking the feed. No giant ad covering the reason people came to WVLRP.

DIRECT LISTENER CONVERSION

Where appropriate, WVLRP can point viewers directly to 98.7's own listening options and mobile app so a WVLRP viewer can become a direct station listener.

CROSS-PROMOTION

We can feature 98.7 naturally across WVLRP, partnership material, social promotion and future outreach while keeping the wildlife experience clean.

The goal is simple: introduce the station to people who are already spending time with a West Virginia experience, then make it easy for them to continue listening directly to 98.7 beyond WVLRP.

What WVLRP brings

WVLRP is being built around free live access to real West Virginia wildlife, multiple camera locations, community interaction and a viewing experience designed to keep people coming back. We want partnerships to feel like part of that world rather than something viewers have to tolerate.

For 98.7, The Roost gives us a particularly natural fit: local wildlife, chickens, live video, a practical reason for radio audio, and an audience that can discover the station simply by enjoying the camera.

Our rule for the partnership

The wildlife always stays first. Any 98.7 promotion on WVLRP should be tasteful, useful and recognizable, but it should never obstruct, interrupt or interfere with the live wildlife video. We think protecting that experience makes the partnership more valuable, because viewers are more likely to stay.

West Virginia watching West Virginia — with 98.7 The Mountain playing along.
