Why The Roost?

Nature has no schedule.
The Roost keeps things moving.

Our wildlife cameras may catch deer, birds, or an unexpected visitor—or a quiet stretch of woods. The Roost adds dependable daily chicken activity, giving visitors another live place to spend time while they wait.

We use radio around the coop as part of our predator-deterrence setup. The proposal is to feature 98.7 around the clock, with music and voices accompanying the chickens and outdoor sounds.

A useful extension for advertisers

The station’s existing commercials would be heard naturally with its proposed Roost audio. Clickable business logos and visual placements would be separate direct WVLRP agreements, coordinated with 98.7.

Room to grow together

Games, wildlife trivia, coffee and community spaces offer future opportunities for participation. Reporting can be scoped around visits, repeat visitors, time spent, and partner-link engagement.
