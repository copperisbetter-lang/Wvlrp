# WVLRP — Tier 4: Exclusive Sitewide & Game/Quest Presentation Sponsor

**Decision recorded:** October 8, 2026. **Status:** Proposed sponsorship inventory, NOT awarded or sold. WVLRP is **soft-launched, not yet publicly launched**.

## Defining promise

**Tier 4 is WVLRP's single highest sponsorship tier. Only ONE sponsor at a time may hold Tier 4. It can ONLY be held under a TWO-YEAR (24-month) sponsorship payment plan.**

**Contractual guarantee:** Once Tier 4 is sold and activated, the sponsor receives **guaranteed logo and brand presentation on EVERY WVLRP page**, including all wildlife pages, 3D/360° locations, community areas, shops, proposal-independent public pages, and **pages primarily sponsored by other businesses**. This is a deliverable, **not merely eligibility or a possibility**. A viewer who looks around enough can find the recognizable sponsor presence, integrated naturally without intrusive pop-ups. Every current page and every new page added during the term must be covered.

**Guaranteed game and quest integration:** The Tier 4 sponsor also receives **logo and brand presentation in ALL relevant WVLRP games and quests** during the contracted term. Integrate the brand through meaningful objects, clues, signs, equipment, mission details, rewards or other context-fitting interactions. “Relevant” means appropriate to the game's or quest's storyline and experience; it does **not** require an unrelated logo forced into every single quest, but for every agreed relevant quest/game the sponsor appearance is a **guaranteed deliverable**, not a discretionary possibility. The contract should define appropriate creative standards and how relevance is decided so there is no ambiguity. These branded features are to be delivered as part of the activation plan, not falsely described as already live.

### Examples of discoverable sitewide presence

- **Base Camp:** a realistic branded object near campsite supplies, engraved logo on gear, or a small meaningful trail sign.
- **Woodland Trail:** branding on a believable trail object, field equipment or ranger note that rewards attentive exploring.
- **The Roost:** an unobtrusive, clickable branded object near the roost or in an optional discovery, while **98.7 The Mountain remains the featured radio partner**.
- **East Bank:** a naturally placed branded item in the 360° environment, while **Moses AutoMall remains visually dominant when demonstrating its page-specific presenting sponsorship**.
- **Ranger Station / quests:** items, clues, correspondence, sponsor-themed discoveries, badges, and appropriate quest interactions.
- **Community, game, sponsor and information pages:** intentional visual or contextual sponsor footprint designed for the page rather than copied generic floating ad.

## Proposed Tier 4 investment range (October 8, 2026)

- **Tier 4 target price range: $80,000–$120,000 per year**, for the sole exclusive sitewide sponsor. This is a **proposed rate range**, not a final quote or a signed agreement.
- **Required 24-month term: $160,000–$240,000 total** at those annual rates over two years, before any separate negotiated extras. Do not present that as a one-year price.
- **The price range is proposed; the final annual rate, payment cadence, deposits, due dates, and any other financial conditions remain to be negotiated.** The two-year commitment itself is mandatory.
- Position this tier as an exclusive, high-value package with guaranteed logo/brand presentation on all WVLRP pages and in all relevant quests and games. As WVLRP has only soft-launched, do not claim a verified audience size, traffic forecast, or return on investment to justify the rate.

## Mandatory two-year payment plan (approved October 8, 2026)

- **Tier 4 is available only on a two-year (24-month) sponsorship payment plan and commitment.** It cannot be held on month-to-month terms, a one-year agreement, or any shorter sponsorship plan.
- The single exclusive Tier 4 sitewide position is reserved for that sponsor during the agreed two-year term, subject to the signed agreement and compliance with payment obligations.
- **The proposed annual price range is $80,000–$120,000; do not invent an agreed final price**, deposit, installments, due dates, financing terms, renewal terms, or cancellation policy. Those details must be negotiated and written into the sponsor agreement before sale.
- A two-year plan describes the required duration and payment-plan structure; whether installments are monthly, quarterly, or otherwise is **not decided yet**.
- This restriction applies to **Tier 4 only**, not the standard payment options for Tiers 1–3. No Tier 4 sponsor has yet been awarded.

## How Tier 4 coexists with other sponsors

- **Exclusivity covers the Tier 4 designation only**: exactly one sitewide discovery sponsor at a time, for an explicitly defined agreement term. It does **not** grant ownership of WVLRP or an exclusive lock on the Roost, East Bank, all quests, or all ad inventory.
- Other companies retain the right to purchase **Tier 1, 2 and 3** placements, with their agreed page identities and prominence intact.
- An East Bank presenting sponsor still leads the East Bank page; 98.7 remains visibly featured on the Roost. The Tier 4 mark/cameo is recognizable but secondary on another company's sponsored page.
- **Tiers 2–3** are eligible for proposed, individually negotiated game and quest appearances. **Tier 4 is different: logo and brand presentation are GUARANTEED on every site page and in all relevant games and quests**, under the agreement and implementation schedule. Other sponsors may also appear in quests; Tier 4 does not own all quest inventory.
- Protect the user experience: don't block gameplay, camera feeds, interactive controls, accessibility or natural imagery. Provide accessible descriptions and mobile-friendly interaction for meaningful clickable placements.
- Treat external sponsor logos, trademarks, products, promotions, prizes and links as requiring relevant permissions, negotiated creative approval and commercial disclosure.

## Delivery and accountability

- **Maintain a page-by-page GUARANTEED Tier 4 logo/brand-presentation checklist** for all existing pages, including pages led by another sponsor. Every newly published page must carry an approved, discoverable Tier 4 treatment. No page may be omitted without a separately negotiated written exception.
- A repeat visitor who explores the area should reasonably be able to discover the Tier 4 sponsor without hunting a single invisible pixel. Some pages may use an overt but tasteful sponsorship mark; others a quest object or scenic Easter egg.
- **Maintain a game-and-quest integration checklist** documenting every relevant game/quest and its promised Tier 4 logo/brand appearance. Relevance and creative format are agreed with the sponsor; all relevant games and quests must be covered as a contractual guarantee. Placements can vary (including context-driven puzzles), but cannot silently disappear.
- For measurable promises, define how sponsor impressions, interactions, clicks and game engagements are counted, subject to privacy and platform limitations. Do not guarantee unmeasured audiences or outcomes.
- A single configuration for the current Tier 4 sponsor and agreement dates should drive all integrated marks so a sponsor change can be managed systematically; no Tier 4 brand should be hardcoded before a deal is approved.
- Final price within the proposed $80,000–$120,000 yearly range, payment installment cadence, exact creative and activation milestones remain to be documented, not invented. **The non-negotiable Tier 4 base deliverables are logo/brand presentation on EVERY page and in ALL relevant quests and games, one Tier 4 holder, and a two-year payment plan**; any written exception must be made explicit before signing.

## Tier 4 sales targeting and proposal policy (October 8, 2026)

- **Present Tier 4 to EVERY major-company or high-investment sponsorship prospect** as an optional, separate upgrade/opportunity alongside their proposed Tier 1–3 package. This includes national corporations, larger regional businesses, major automotive dealers, and major media/strategic partnership proposals. Existing examples include **Moses AutoMall** and the substantial **98.7 The Mountain** proposal; future outreach to **Cabela's / Bass Pro Shops** or similarly sized prospects should include it as well.
- **Do NOT pitch Tier 4 in routine mom-and-pop, small-local-business, restaurant, or entry-tier proposals.** Focus those conversations on appropriately priced Tier 1–3 choices so a $80K–$120K/year headline does not intimidate, discourage, or overwhelm smaller partners.
- Tier 4 is a **visible but clearly optional flagship upgrade** in major proposals. Always showcase the prospect's tailored core sponsorship first; then introduce the exclusive sitewide upgrade in its own readable section or page, never implying that the core package requires Tier 4.
- Explain: one slot worldwide across WVLRP; guaranteed logo and brand presentation on all current/future pages (including other-sponsored pages); guaranteed presence in relevant games and quests; mandatory two-year payment plan; **proposed $80,000–$120,000 per year ($160,000–$240,000 for two years)**, final details by agreement.
- The site is **only soft-launched; full public launch has not occurred**. Do not claim existing commitments, guaranteed traffic, already deployed branded quest placements, or an awarded Tier 4.
- Tier 4 availability must be checked before offering exclusivity; once sold, subsequent large proposals should explain the slot is occupied rather than promising it again.
- **Audience suitability matters:** A sponsor receiving a major pitch can still select a narrower option without pressure. Smaller operators may request information about Tier 4 voluntarily, but are not proactively presented with the high-price package in their standard pitch.
