# WVLRP — Two-Minute Sponsor Presentation (Production Board v1)
**Project:** West Virginia Live Research Project (WVLRP)  
**Budget:** $0 in paid video-generation subscriptions  
**Status:** Draft screenplay / edit plan. Neither the complete video nor the planned Adventure features are presented as finished.  
**Destination:** Private sponsor previews, first suitable for Cabela's, Moses Auto Mall and 98.7 The Mountain; create a neutral film first, then customize ending/cards per partner.

## Editorial rules
- 00:00–00:59 is **actual site and field footage**, clearly sourced from WVLRP where available. No synthetic images masquerading as live camera footage.
- 01:00–01:59 is an **illustrative Adventure concept preview** assembled from existing WVLRP game locations + AI-generated camera motion. Place a discreet but readable "Adventure concept preview • features in development" label across that segment.
- Do not claim operational features, animal species, audience figures, visitor numbers or camera reliability that have not been verified.
- The production master is landscape 1920×1080, 30 fps, MP4/H.264, with optional vertical 9:16 cut later. AI input previews are lower resolution and should be replaced or upscaled only when quality warrants.
- Wild animal scenes should show animals behaving naturally, never the impression of a visitor feeding or approaching them.
- No use of footage with forbidden removed watermarks. Free, appropriately licensed generation or our own originals only.

### Audio/editor notes
- First minute: natural ambience from genuine footage at a low level; restrained music and confident warm human narration.
- At 01:00: take tonal inspiration from classic mysterious-TV opening narration (such as Tales from the Darkside) ONLY in pacing and atmosphere—never imitate copyrighted script, soundtrack, or voice. Real camera dissolves into dark, glowing tree silhouettes, foliage opens to our colorful real Adventure artwork; keep the film intriguing, welcoming and sponsor-appropriate rather than scary.
- At 01:00: nature ambience briefly deepens, music lifts, camera crosses through foliage into the concept experience.
- Avoid making AI narration sound like a corporate email; use conversational, unhurried delivery.
- A 60-second concept reel may require 10–15 generated clips; static shots and close-ups can reduce the number.
- Keep source assets, license evidence and rendered MP4s in a separate research branch until approved for public/sponsor release.

3. Verify generated-source terms allow commercial sponsorship use without watermark manipulation.
4. Review a rough cut at 120 seconds, then create sponsor-specific version with permission and branding.
