# WVLRP — Sponsorship phase checkpoint (October 8, 2026)

**Status:** WVLRP is in the **sponsorship and partnership development phase**. The project **has soft-launched only** and **has NOT had its full public launch**. This distinction must be clearly disclosed to **every prospective sponsor**, in outreach, presentation pages, decks, and proposals. Do not imply the site is fully publicly launched, that a partnership has been signed, or that future audiences/views are guaranteed.

## Standard sponsor-facing launch disclosure

> **WVLRP is currently soft-launched and preparing for its full public launch.** This preview offers prospective partners an early look at the live wildlife and immersive visitor experiences, with an opportunity to be involved ahead of the wider public rollout.

Keep this prominent and readable in each proposed-sponsor experience, not hidden in tiny footer text. Refer to real audience metrics only when measured, and label projections as projections. Private proposal examples must remain clearly identified as proposals, not active endorsements.

## The Roost — strategic sponsorship rules (agreed)

- The Roost is expected to be one of WVLRP's most frequently visited destinations because the chickens provide recurring activity throughout the day. This is a **hypothesis**, not a measured traffic claim yet.
- **Do not give 98.7 The Mountain, or any single modest-budget sponsor, blanket exclusive sponsorship rights to the entire Roost.**
- **98.7 The Mountain:** offer a prominent, generous position as the **Featured Radio Partner**. The existing concept includes a large visible station logo, radio integration, app-download button/QR, Meet the 98.7 Crew, DJ-led contests, and Roost-specific collaborations.
- The production idea is to show chickens **roosting at night**, then show the **run and grazing activity in the morning** via suitable camera placement/pan. The page's immersive view and station identity should support each other.
- For the proposal demonstration, a clean actual 98.7 broadcast was requested instead of noisy camera audio. Confirm streaming permissions, rights, and approved technical integration before offering this as a live public feature.
- **Keep room for major corporate investors** to buy substantial, highly visible placements on the Roost **alongside** 98.7, plus selectively placed smaller sponsors.
- If offered at all, 98.7's exclusivity could be **radio-category-specific** and expressly limited by contract. No exclusive ownership or control over the Roost page or WVLRP inventory.
- Any sponsor contract must preserve WVLRP's ability to add compatible sponsors, show its own content, and expand monetization. Avoid clutter: distinguish Featured Radio Partner / Premier Corporate Sponsor / Supporting Partner visually.
- Measure Roost visits, repeat sessions, dwell time, sponsor impressions, engagement, and outbound clicks before valuing inventory based on audience size.
