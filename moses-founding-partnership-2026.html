01 · Purpose and community

Nature brings people in. Community keeps them coming back.

Our goal at the West Virginia Live Research Project is to give people free, authentic ways to experience the West Virginia outdoors, then connect with others who share that interest. The wildlife cameras provide the spark. Conversations, friendships, and a welcoming community create a lasting destination.

This vision draws on experience with our predecessor, KLRP. Its founder recalls that viewers gathered in ten chat rooms, with as many as 1,200–1,500 people participating in chats simultaneously. These are historical founder-reported figures, not verified current WVLRP audience statistics. More important than the numbers were the friendships: viewers who originally arrived for cameras returned to visit one another.

WVLRP plans public community rooms with moderation and member-created, invitation-only rooms. Chat creators would decide who can invite other people. Our intention is to help visitors find smaller, familiar groups and remain connected across different camera pages—and, eventually, together inside The Wild itself.

We intend to keep wildlife viewing uninterrupted by video advertisements. Business supporters would be recognized around the camera pages and through optional activities, rather than cutting away from an animal in the middle of a moment.

03 · Our near-term and long-term goals

Build something reliable now—and meaningful for years.

Short term · 6–12 months

A dependable foundation

  • Keep existing live cameras reliable, with better monitoring and backup power.
  • Invest in high-definition wildlife-viewing cameras and high-quality stereo audio capture, so visitors can enjoy clearer images and more immersive natural sound as equipment and bandwidth allow.
  • Acquire wireless signal repeater equipment, point-to-point radio bridges and supporting field power hardware to extend dependable camera connections deeper into the woods, subject to terrain and site surveys.
  • Improve streaming capacity and mobile viewing, and launch additional cameras as ready.
  • Develop public and invitation-only chats, member tools and moderation.
  • Build community interest responsibly and begin reporting real audience engagement.
Long term · 1–3 years and beyond

A West Virginia destination

  • Expand to HD camera coverage and immersive stereo wildlife sound across more field locations, as infrastructure and funding allow.
  • Build a network of strategically placed wireless relays and, where needed, off-grid power systems so future wildlife cameras can reach more remote wooded areas.
  • More wildlife locations, educational features and seasonal events.
  • Growing member friendships and a larger network of community gathering spaces.
  • Grow The Wild as a social gathering place: shared quests and mini-games, relaxing together at the lake, real-time conversations and personalized player avatars.
  • Develop age-appropriate family and child-safe activities, with suitable moderation, privacy safeguards and protected social features.
  • Teach West Virginia history, wildlife, geography and natural beauty through exploration, stories, educational games and quests.
  • Continue expanding the natural world with animals, trails, vehicles, fishing and adventures, including interactive hilltop viewing scopes that help friends discover scenic vistas and locations.
  • Innovative sponsor opportunities as the project's reach and features expand.
04 · The Wild · Our next generation of community

Not simply a game. A place to meet, explore and belong.

The Wild is our ambitious West Virginia-inspired virtual world in development. Its purpose goes far beyond completing a game level: we envision an online destination where friends can spend meaningful time together, families can share adventures, and visitors can experience the history, wildlife, and natural beauty of West Virginia in a completely new way.

Someone might log in to join friends on a woodland quest, spend an evening fishing beside a lake, or simply sit together on the beach and have a real conversation. Those casual moments of connection are as important to us as the challenges and adventures. The same community-first philosophy that shaped our earlier live-camera project will shape The Wild.

Real social experiences

Friends can share the world

Meet at lakeside gathering places, sit together at the beach, explore forests and waterfalls, fish, and enjoy real-time conversations. The Wild is intended to be a place people can return to just to spend time with their friends.

Adventure together

Cooperative quests and mini-games

Work with friends to solve mysteries, complete shared quests, discover hidden places and enjoy smaller activities. Players should have reasons to collaborate, help one another and celebrate accomplishments together.

Personal expression

Avatars that feel like you

Customize your character's appearance, clothing and style to reflect your own personality. The goal is to give members a sense of identity and belonging, rather than forcing everyone into the same generic character.

Family and child safety

Age-appropriate places to play

Develop welcoming child-safe experiences with suitable mini-quests, games and protected social features. Age-appropriate character designs, moderation, reporting, privacy controls and meaningful safeguards will be part of the design before child-oriented social features are launched.

Education through discovery

Experience Wild and Wonderful West Virginia

Use adventures to introduce the state's history, communities, wildlife, landscapes and natural wonders. Instead of only reading a fact on a page, a player could learn through a guided challenge, a place-based story, an exploration activity or an educational quest.

A world that grows

Trails, wildlife and new discoveries

Ongoing development includes a lakeside base camp, forest trails, water features, fishing, wildlife, characters and story quests. Future areas can introduce new environments, activities, seasonal gatherings and ways for friends to explore together.

Development status: The Wild is still being built and tested. These social, cooperative, educational and child-safety features describe our plans; they are not claims that full public multiplayer, voice chat, child accounts or every mini-game is currently available.

An authentic place for Moses in The Wild

Subtle advertising that belongs in the scenery—not on top of the experience.

Moses Auto Group could help bring this shared West Virginia world to life and receive tasteful recognition along the way. Instead of putting sponsor signs along wilderness trails, Moses could be part of places where automotive businesses naturally fit: a vehicle dealership or service hub in a future town area, an optional scenic-driving experience, or a realistic dealership detail connected to a vehicle. We want the woods to feel like woods. Sponsorship should be discovered naturally, never interrupt conversations, quests or wildlife viewing.

Natural automotive setting

Vehicle and service details

Small, believable details where they belong: a Moses keychain, a dealership emblem on a parked vehicle at the village lodge, a stack of Moses business cards on the lodge desk, or an unobtrusive license-plate frame—rather than signs scattered through woodland trails.

Future gathering destination

Moses Motor Outpost

A modest vehicle-related service or meeting place in a future village area. Smaller details would be available for negotiation in Tier 2; a named Moses AutoMall quest destination and storyline are exclusively Tier 3.

Optional shared experiences

West Virginia scenic routes

Friends could choose to explore a sponsored driving route or a vehicle-related event, without any requirement to view an ad before playing.

Exclusive signature experience · Tier 3 Premium only

The Moses Scenic Overlook

Imagine friends hiking to a hilltop overlook and finding a traditional long-distance viewing telescope. Turn the viewer toward one direction to discover the East Bank observation area—and potentially open a live view from the real East Bank wildlife camera. Pan toward another compass direction, and a recognizable Moses AutoMall appears on the distant West Virginia-inspired horizon.

This would be an optional, immersive discovery, not an advertisement that interrupts play. The named overlook and featured distant dealership landmark are reserved exclusively for our highest Tier 3 Signature Automotive Partner—not included in Tier 1, Tier 2, or standard game advertising. The concept is a planned experience, subject to technical feasibility, environment design, brand approval and an agreed delivery milestone.

Exclusive playable storyline · Tier 3 Premium only

A quest that leads to Moses AutoMall

Imagine friends finding a missing clue or needing a particular part during an adventure. The quest sends them to a virtual Moses AutoMall within The Wild, where they speak with a character, receive information, examine a map or collect an item that moves the story forward. What starts as a natural stop in the story becomes a memorable location players actually visit and talk about.

This Moses AutoMall quest and named location are exclusively reserved for Tier 3, alongside the scenic overlook. A player would never need to visit a real dealership, make a purchase, or watch a commercial to advance. The storyline, assets and any family-friendly alternatives would be designed and approved before launch.

Exclusive sponsor-created adventures · Tier 3 Premium only

Quests written by Moses, brought to life in The Wild

Our highest-tier partner could request and write an agreed number of original quests during the sponsorship term. Moses could provide the stories, characters, dialogue, clues, goals, items, and preferred locations—not merely choose from quests we have already made. WVLRP would collaborate with Moses to fit those concepts naturally into the world, then build, test, and release the approved adventures as development allows.

The number of quests, size and complexity of each quest, review process, milestones, and rights to submitted creative material would be agreed in writing before development. Quests would be age-appropriate where offered to children, support The Wild's exploration and educational goals, and never require a purchase, physical dealership visit, or forced advertisement.

Moses would review and approve proposed sponsor creative. Vehicle brands, models and trademarks would require appropriate permissions. Any in-world sponsorship would be tailored to the audience and would not be used for targeted advertising within child-directed experiences. Specific assets, features and timelines would be agreed before they become contract commitments.

06 · Funding we need to move forward

Our first-year founding partnership goal: $30,000.

A $30,000 annual partnership would help WVLRP fund the essentials of dependable operations and measured growth: acquiring high-definition wildlife-viewing cameras and quality stereo microphones; installing wireless repeaters, long-range point-to-point links and suitable remote power equipment so cameras can operate deeper in the woods; upgrading field equipment, power protection, reliable streaming and internet backup, improving security and moderation for community tools, and developing The Wild's shared social experiences, age-appropriate mini-quests, customizable avatars and West Virginia educational adventures in stages. These improvements are future goals, not claims about the resolution or audio quality of our current live streams.

Our preference is a committed first-year agreement with upfront funding, if mutually acceptable, so we can complete the most urgent infrastructure work without relying on uncertain monthly donations. Sponsorship placements, partnership benefits, agreement terms and reporting commitments will be clearly defined before signing; day-to-day operating and equipment-purchasing decisions remain with WVLRP.

This is a commercial marketing partnership, not a request for a charitable tax-deductible gift. We do not promise future audience counts, traffic, sales, or a completed game feature that has not yet launched.
