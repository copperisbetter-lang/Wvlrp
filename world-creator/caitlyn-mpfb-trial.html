What this is: a separate adult MakeHuman/MPFB-generated character with wavy platinum-blonde hair, a continuous, complexion-matched face/neck/body material and a subtle smile morph, and a preserved full-body base mesh. The poses are for preview testing; they have not been verified as physically realistic. The model has NOT been verified for calibrated measurements, and it is not plugged into gameplay movement yet. Other characters and the existing world map have not been changed.
