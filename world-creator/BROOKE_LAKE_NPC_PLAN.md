# Brooke lake NPC — approved behavior (October 22, 2026)

Brooke is a non-quest ambient adult NPC. This is a design plan, not implemented gameplay.

## Full character
Build a recognizable, realistic full-body character with reference-guided face, blonde hair, skin, shoulders, torso, bust, waist, hips, buttocks, legs and natural motion. Use multiple photos to mitigate perspective distortion. Keep proportions adjustable in a dedicated Brooke preset.

## Lake behavior
- Initially sunbathes on a rock on the player-accessible beach side.
- When a player approaches, sits up, smiles, stands, jumps into lake, and swims at a natural speed across to a second rock on the far shore.
- Climbs out, waves, lies down and resumes sunbathing.
- The far rock is protected by an invisible collision/navigation barrier; players can swim nearby but cannot reach her. Brooke's own swim path remains within her designated space.
- When no players remain in the beach or surrounding lake area, wait roughly 30 seconds to avoid oscillation, then swim naturally back to the original rock and resume sunbathing.
- Multiplayer should share one consistent NPC state and animation. No quests or dialogue trees.

## Video reference and animation
User reports substantial online video of Brooke in swimwear, swimming, jumping into the water and climbing out. Locate and assess specific clips before using them as reference; links and usage rights still need verification. Analyze swimming strokes, posture, water entry and exit, natural motion, wet hair behavior and multiple camera angles. Animate transitions between sunbathing, sitting up, smiling, standing, jumping, swimming, climbing out, waving and lying down. Support dry, wet and damp hair appearances and appropriate water effects.
