Founding partnership opportunities · West Virginia

Help build a lasting window into the Wild and Wonderful.

The West Virginia Live Research Project (WVLRP) is developing free public wildlife observation, live field cameras and nature-based digital experiences. We invite local businesses to help us grow from a working project into a dependable community destination.

01 / Our mission

Let people discover the outdoors, as it happens.

WVLRP is being built around a simple idea: the woods are more interesting when people can observe them for themselves. We aim to connect viewers with real West Virginia landscapes and wildlife through accessible camera views, thoughtful field information, and interactive experiences inspired by the natural world.

Our goal is for visitors to watch without a required paid membership. Sponsorships would support the equipment, connections, maintenance and development that make that possible.

03 / The long-term vision

More than one camera. A growing West Virginia experience.

More reliable field observation

Strengthen stream uptime and maintenance, then add new observation views as equipment and connectivity permit. Make wildlife-watching easier to enjoy across phones and other devices.

Learning and exploration

Develop wildlife notes, nature-related information and digital exploration experiences. The Wild game and other interactive experiences are separate, developing parts of our broader creative vision.

How a sponsorship discussion works

We begin with a conversation about shared goals, then provide a tailored proposal covering deliverables, rates, duration and payment terms. No sponsorship package, logo placement or public audience size is guaranteed until it is documented and accepted.

WVLRP is still preparing wider promotion and does not yet present verified audience or impression figures. Businesses can review the project and its planned development before deciding whether to participate.
